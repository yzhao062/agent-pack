# Venue Calibration

Venue criteria and score labels change. Verify the current cycle from the venue's official reviewer guide before dispatching the panel. Use primary venue pages, not blog summaries.

## Calibration procedure

1. Confirm the venue and submission cycle.
2. Open the official reviewer guide and, when relevant, area-chair guidance.
3. Extract the allowed overall-score values, labels, confidence scale, and review dimensions.
4. Note venue-specific policies that affect evaluation, such as contemporaneous work, anonymity, reproducibility, ethics, or artifact expectations.
5. Put the exact scale and links in the neutral reviewer prompt.

If the official scale or confidence rubric cannot be verified, do not guess from memory. Produce the technical review without a numeric score and ask for the venue guidance or permission to use a clearly labeled provisional scale.

## Common dimensions

Most research venues evaluate some combination of:

- technical soundness and support for claims;
- novelty or new knowledge;
- significance and likely value to the venue community;
- clarity and positioning against related work;
- reproducibility and transparency;
- ethics, limitations, and responsible disclosure when applicable.

Use the venue's own terms when they differ.

## ICLR calibration

Retrieve the current cycle's overall-score values, labels, confidence rubric, and review dimensions from the official ICLR reviewer guide before constructing the neutral brief. Do not carry a score list from this reference into a live review.

For ICLR, ask whether the work adds sufficient new knowledge or value, is well motivated and situated, rigorously supports its claims, and is significant enough for the conference. Do not penalize a paper solely for failing to cite very recent or contemporaneous work when the current venue policy protects against that use.

## Cross venue rule

Do not map scores mechanically across venues. A weak accept at one venue is not automatically the same decision at another. Re-run the significance and audience-fit judgment under the named venue's criteria while keeping the technical audit fixed.
